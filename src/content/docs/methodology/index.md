This section describes the decision analysis methodology that prisma-decision
builds on. It is organised in two parts:

- **DA/DQ** — an introduction to decision analysis (DA) and decision quality
  (DQ) as applied in prisma-decision. *This document will be added later.*
- **Technical notes** — focused notes on the
  concepts, models and algorithms used by prisma-decision: influence diagrams,
  decision trees, partial orders, arc reversal, the pyagrum solver, and more.
