## Delete an arc

For an arc into a decision or uncertainty, open its restriction table and select the trash icon. For an arc into a Utility issue, select the trash icon shown when hovering over the arc. Check the source and target in the **Delete Edge** confirmation, then select **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep it. Deleting the arc removes the relationship and its restrictions; review affected probability or utility inputs and results afterward.
