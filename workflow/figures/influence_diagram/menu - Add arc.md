## Add an arc

In **Shape view**, hover over the source issue and select the **+** control on its right. Choose a target from **Add arc to**; the arrow points from source to target. Alternatively, drag from the source control to the target node.
In **Diagram view**, click and drag from the tail node to the head node.

Add only a relationship supported by the model: an arrow into a decision represents information available before that decision. Check the target's probability or utility inputs after changing its parents.
