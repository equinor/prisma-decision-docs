## Check branch values near a leaf

On uncertainty branches, **Prob** is the probability of that outcome, shown as a decimal, and **Utility** is the utility assigned to the branch. In this example, `0.25 x 100 + 0.50 x 75 + 0.25 x 25 = 68.75`, matching the **EV** beside the uncertainty node. At each leaf, **Value** is the terminal value and **Cumulative probability** is the probability of the full path. Expand only as far as needed to check a path.
