# Prisma workflow

These page guides explain the possibilities available in Prisma, from framing a
decision to reviewing its model and assessing decision quality. Follow the
sequence below as a starting point, not a mandatory checklist: revisit earlier
work as new insights emerge, and use only the tools relevant to your decision.
Assess decision quality throughout the process, not just at the end.

## Before you start

Use the sign-in method configured for your Prisma deployment and confirm that
you have access to the intended project. In the [demo version](https://prisma-demo.equinor.com/login.html), use non-personal training
identifiers and non-confidential data. Management controls depend on project
roles, so the facilitator's screenshots may show more buttons than a member sees.

Expand the sidebar to read the workflow labels while learning the application.
Changing light/dark mode affects presentation, not project inputs or results.

## Home page

[Home page](./pages/home_page.md): create or import a project, find existing
   projects, and manage favorites.

## Frame the decision

- [Project information](./pages/project_information.md): state the opportunity, review the team, and manage settings.
- [Stakeholder matrix](./pages/stakeholder_matrix.md): identify stakeholders and discuss their influence and how they are affected.
- [Objectives](./pages/objectives.md): define, classify, and refine what the decision should achieve.
- [Issues](./pages/issues.md): capture and classify issues and define the model boundary.
- [Strategies](./pages/strategies.md): combine decision options into courses of action
   and compare the combinations.
- [Whiteboard](./pages/whiteboard.md): draft ideas, including the collaborative exploration and organization of issues.

## Structure and explore the model

- [Influence diagram](./pages/influence_diagram.md): inspect the model structure,
   probability inputs, and calculated results. It includes what-if scenarios.
- Trees
   - [Decision tree](./pages/decision_tree.md): expand branches to explore sequences of decisions and uncertain outcomes.
   - [Compact tree](./pages/compact_tree.md): inspect an alternative, condensed tree view (time sequence of events).
   - [Solution tree](./pages/solution_tree.md): examine the solution representation.

The compact and solution trees are alternative views, not additional required
model-building steps.

## Assess and iterate

- [Assessments](./pages/assessments.md): create evaluation rounds, submit scores,
    review responses, and track progress.

Use [Assess decision quality](./pages/decision_quality.md) for the six elements,
discussion prompts, and scoring guidance. Return to framing or model development
when an assessment identifies weaknesses that could change the decision.

## Before sharing a decision record

- Confirm the opportunity, objectives, boundaries, and decision authority.
- Check that arrows on the influence diagram represent the intended dependencies and information timing.
- Review probability row sums, value inputs, units, and all required state
  combinations.
- Discuss expected value and downside rather than presenting an average as a
  guaranteed outcome.
